Abstract
With the increasing number of items requiring handling simultaneously in complex logistics, offline three-dimensional
packing methods need to plan larger numbers of items. Existing deep reinforcement learning (DRL)-based packing methods cannot plan for large numbers of items while keeping
high-quality solutions due to limited exploration space and
high computational complexity. To address this issue, this
paper proposes a scalable DRL-based packing method. An
attention-based pack-Q-network (PQNet) is constructed to
learn the optimal packing policy by integrating unpacked
items, available spaces, and packed items. To expand the valid
exploration space, a bidding-based multi-policy (BBMP)
framework composed of multiple PQNets is designed to efficiently explore more latent valid solutions, thus enhancing solution quality. To reduce computational complexity,
a training-free dynamic candidate selection (DCS) framework is proposed to incorporate comprehensive item information during execution with minimal computation overhead, which helps in effectively planning large numbers of
items. Experimental results show that across item numbers
of 20∼1000, our method consistently outperforms the bestperforming baseline at each tested scale by 3.2%∼13.1% in
space utilization.

Introduction
The offline three-dimensional packing problem (3D-PP) is
a practical combinatorial optimization problem widely studied in logistics, warehousing, and manufacturing (Lin et al.
2024; Yao et al. 2025). As real-world applications grow
more complex, the number of items that need to be planned
for 3D-PP has significantly increased. There is an urgent demand for a scalable packing method to solve offline 3D-PP
involving large numbers of items.
The offline 3D-PP is known as an NP-hard problem (Bortfeldt and Wascher 2013), focusing on packing a set of ¨
cuboid-shaped items into cuboid-shaped containers, aiming
to maximize the space utilization. The NP-hard nature of
the problem prevents it from being solved exactly, as exact
mathematical methods (Martello, Pisinger, and Vigo 2000;

Tsai, Wang, and Lin 2015) often suffer from heavy time
consumption and suboptimal probabilities of finding feasible solutions. Hence, using heuristics to obtain approximate
solutions within reduced time has been a predominant approach (Ali et al. 2022; Yang et al. 2024; Heßler, Hintsch,
and Wienkamp 2025). However, heuristics usually rely on
handcrafted rules, which limits their performance and makes
them poorly adaptable to diverse packing scenarios (Pan,
Chen, and Lin 2023).
Recently, the powerful decision-making capability of
deep reinforcement learning (DRL) has attracted studies to
explore its application in offline 3D-PP (Hu et al. 2017;
Duan et al. 2019). As a key advantage over conventional
methods, DRL enables policy exploration during training,
thereby obtaining high-quality solutions with minimal overhead during execution (Liu et al. 2023). Moreover, guided
by appropriate reward signals, the agent can learn underlying packing patterns to adapt to various packing scenarios
with improved performance. However, developing effective
and scalable DRL-based packing methods remains challenging. On the one hand, the agent’s large action space for item
placement causes difficulties in policy exploration, leading
to degraded solution quality (Zhao et al. 2021). The dominant approach for this issue is to integrate heuristics to mask
out unreasonable placement (Yang et al. 2023; Que, Yang,
and Zhang 2023; Xu et al. 2023; Yin, Chen, and He 2024;
Xiong et al. 2024; Wang et al. 2025), thus compressing the
action space and enhancing exploration efficiency. However,
such a heuristic-driven DRL paradigm explores within the
narrow space limited by heuristic rules, which results in the
neglect of many latent valid solutions and makes the agent
prone to getting stuck in local optima. On the other hand,
large numbers of items increase memory usage and computational complexity, as existing well-performing DRL-based
methods primarily use attention mechanisms (Vaswani et al.
2017) to encode items, whose computational overhead is
susceptible to the sequence length.
To address the above issues, this paper proposes an effective and scalable DRL-based method for solving the offline 3D-PP. Firstly, we develop an attention-based packQ-network (PQNet) to capture the dependencies among information regarding unpacked items, available spaces, and
packed items to derive action values. Secondly, we propose a
bidding-based multi-policy (BBMP) framework that considThe Fortieth AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence (AAAI-26)

ers multiple packing directions to enrich the packing policy
and explore more valid solutions while keeping high exploration efficiency. Finally, we divide all unpacked items into a
processing set and a candidate set. A training-free dynamic
candidate selection (DCS) framework is proposed to move
the optimal candidate item into the processing set using the
pre-trained BBMP model parameters. DCS framework incorporates comprehensive item information during execution with minimal computation overhead, thereby enhancing performance for large numbers of items. Extensive experimental results show that the proposed method achieves
state-of-the-art performance on 3D-PP with 20∼1000 items.
The method also exhibits generalization capabilities across
varying container sizes and item numbers.
Related Works
Throughout the evolution of 3D-PP, numerous methods have
emerged, which can be broadly categorized into five classes.
1) Exact algorithms (Chen, Lee, and Shen 1995; Martello,
Pisinger, and Vigo 2000) and 2) approximation algorithms
(Chung, Garey, and Johnson 1982; Galvez et al. 2021) fo- ´
cus on theoretically ensuring optimal and near-optimal solutions, but they are limited by high computational complexity
and poor scalability for large-scale instances.
3) Constructive heuristics aim to find feasible solutions
based on handcrafted rules, which usually place items into
spaces based on loading patterns (George and Robinson
1980; Toffolo et al. 2017; Saraiva, Nepomuceno, and Pinheiro 2015; Yang et al. 2024), with these spaces being managed using specialized techniques (Bortfeldt, Gehring, and
Mack 2003; Crainic, Perboli, and Tadei 2008; Parreno et al. ˜
2008). However, the reliance on handcrafted rules makes
them struggle to adapt to scenario changes.
4) Search algorithms attempt to explore search spaces
while adhering to constructive heuristic rules. Metaheuristic methods (Liu et al. 2011; Gonc¸alves and Resende
2013; Liu et al. 2017; Shao and Xiao 2025) are commonly
used to improve the quality of complete solutions iteratively.
Tree search methods (Ren, Tian, and Sawaragi 2011) create
solution branches based on the process of packing and selecting the best one among them. These methods are effective in finding high-quality solutions but come at the cost of
significant time inefficiency.
5) Deep reinforcement learning (DRL)-based methods are
a class of methods that emerged recently as the flourishes of
DRL. (Hu et al. 2017; Duan et al. 2019) first used the pointer
network (Vinyals, Fortunato, and Jaitly 2015) as the policy
network to solve the offline 3D-PP for minimizing the surface area of the bounding cuboid. Conditional query learning
(CQL) (Li et al. 2020) solves the offline 3D-PP to minimize
the variable container height (named 3D strip packing problem (3D-SPP)), by embedding previous actions as a conditional query to the attention model. Building upon CQL,
recurrent CQL (RCQL) (Li et al. 2022) further manages a
candidate queue with the head-of-queue items being moved
to the processing set at each time step, thus scaling the number of planable items to thousands. (Jiang, Cao, and Zhang
2023) improved CQL by introducing the sparse attention
mechanism to reduce computational complexity. The action
representation learning was employed to handle the large
action space for placement. (Que, Yang, and Zhang 2023)
learned a Transformer-based policy network to guide packing. A plane feature was introduced to represent the bin state
and compressed the action space by heuristically downsampling these features. (Yin, Chen, and He 2024) constructed
a two-stage policy network and heuristically enforced item
packing along two orthogonal directions to compress the action space and enhance exploration efficiency. Most DRLbased methods cannot plan for large numbers of items while
keeping high-quality solutions due to limited exploration
space and high computational complexity. In this paper, we
aim to propose an effective and scalable DRL-based method
to address this issue.

Bidding-Based Multi-Policy Framework
The policy learned by a single PQNet has limitations because it dictates that each item must be placed at one corner
of the space, resulting in the neglect of many valid solutions.
In fact, the four corners of the bottom of the spaces should all
be able to be placed. To explore more latent valid solutions,
we propose a bidding-based multi-policy (BBMP) framework to allocate four policies to four PQNets, where each
network is responsible for estimating the action values for
packing items into one of the corners of spaces: the behindleft-bottom corner, the front-left-bottom corner, the frontright-bottom corner, and the behind-right-bottom corner. To
avoid the non-stationary problem and conflicts among four
policies, two special schemes are implemented: parameter
sharing and action bidding.

Conclusion
This paper proposes a scalable DRL-based method to solve
the offline 3D-PP of diverse scales. The proposed method
demonstrates superior space utilization performance across
various scales of 3D-PP tasks compared to existing methods, attributed to its three core components: PQNet, BBMP
framework, and DCS framework. Furthermore, the solution
exhibits robust generalization to varying container sizes and
item numbers. Challenges remain in handling real-world dynamic online packing scenarios. Extending the framework to
incorporate realistic constraints and adaptive reward mechanisms can further improve its applicability.

class BBMP_DCS:
    def __init__(self, args):
        self.args = args
        self.dqn = DQN(args)
        self.dataset = ItemDataset(args)
        self.dataloader = DataLoader(self.dataset,
                                     batch_size=self.args.col_batch_size,
                                     shuffle=True,
                                     num_workers=0)

        self.device = torch.device('cuda:' + self.args.gpu_ids) if self.args.gpu_ids != '-1' else torch.device('cpu')
        if args.load_model:
            self.dqn.pred_net.load_state_dict(torch.load(args.model_path))
            self.dqn.target_net.load_state_dict(torch.load(args.model_path))
        self.max_u = 0
        self.train_step = 0

    def train(self, ckpt_dir):

        for epoch in range(self.args.train_epochs):
            iterator = tqdm(self.dataloader, unit='Batch')
            for i_batch, sample_batched in enumerate(iterator):
                iterator.set_description('epoch %i, episode %i' % (epoch, i_batch))
                item = sample_batched
                state_proc_item = item[:, 0: args.item_num - args.cand_item_num, :].to(self.device)
                if args.cand_item_num != 0:
                    state_cand_item = item[:, -args.cand_item_num:, :].to(self.device)
                else:
                    state_cand_item = torch.zeros([args.col_batch_size, 1, 3]).to(self.device)

                max_H = torch.sum(torch.max(item, dim=2)[0], dim=1)
                bin = torch.tensor([self.args.bin_length, self.args.bin_width]).unsqueeze(0).repeat(self.args.col_batch_size, 1)
                state_space = torch.cat((torch.zeros([self.args.col_batch_size, 3]), bin, max_H.unsqueeze(1), bin, max_H.unsqueeze(1)), dim=1).unsqueeze(1)
                state_space = torch.cat((state_space, torch.zeros([self.args.col_batch_size, self.args.max_deal_space_num - 1, 9])), dim=1).to(self.device)
                state_pack = torch.zeros([self.args.col_batch_size, min(args.item_num, args.max_deal_pack_num), 9], device=self.device)
                state_pack_col = torch.zeros([self.args.col_batch_size, args.item_num, 9], device=self.device)

                '''
                state_proc_item: (l, w, h) for each item in processing set
                state_cand_item: (l, w, h) for each item in candidate set
                state_pack: (x, y, z, x', y', w', l', w', h') for each item in packed set
                state_pack_col: (x, y, z, x', y', w', l', w', h') for each item in full packed set
                state_space: (x, y, z, x', y', w', l', w', h') for each space
                '''

                for step in range(self.args.item_num):
                    action_, action_size = self.dqn.choose_action(state_proc_item, state_space, state_pack, evaluate=False)
                    id = (action_ / action_size).long()
                    action = action_ % action_size
                    idx_io = (action / state_space.size(1)).long()
                    idx_s = (action % state_space.size(1)).long()
                    state_packs = self.dqn.rotate_pack(state_pack)
                    state_packs_col = self.dqn.rotate_pack(state_pack_col)
                    state_spaces = self.dqn.rotate_space(state_space)

                    state_pack = torch.gather(state_packs,
                                               0,
                                               (id % 4).view(1, -1, 1, 1).repeat(1, 1, state_packs.size(2), state_packs.size(3))).squeeze(0)
                    state_pack_col = torch.gather(state_packs_col,
                                              0,
                                              (id % 4).view(1, -1, 1, 1).repeat(1, 1, state_packs_col.size(2),
                                                                                state_packs_col.size(3))).squeeze(0)
                    state_space = torch.gather(state_spaces,
                                              0,
                                              (id % 4).view(1, -1, 1, 1).repeat(1, 1, state_spaces.size(2), state_spaces.size(3))).squeeze(0)


                    state_proc_item_, state_space_, state_pack_, state_pack_col_ = Pack.update_state(state_proc_item,
                                                                                                      state_space,
                                                                                                      state_pack,
                                                                                                      state_pack_col,
                                                                                                      action)
                    # action_cand = self.dqn.chose_action_cand(state_proc_item_, state_pack_, state_space_, state_cand_item)
                    action_cand = None
                    state_proc_item_, state_cand_item_ = Pack.update_state_cand(state_proc_item_, state_space, state_cand_item, action, action_cand)
                    rewards = Pack.cal_reward(state_pack_col, state_pack_col_, self.args.bin_length, self.args.bin_width)

                    if torch.count_nonzero(state_proc_item_) == 0:
                        V = torch.sum(torch.prod(state_pack_col_[:, :, 6:9], 2), dim=1)
                        H = torch.max(state_pack_col_[:, :, 5], dim=1)[0]
                        L, W = args.bin_length, args.bin_width
                        g = V / (L * W * H)
                        if self.train_step > 0 and args.use_tensorboard:
                            writer.add_scalar(
                                'data/utilization_{}_{}_{}_{}'.format(args.bin_length, args.bin_width, args.item_num, args.cand_item_num),
                                g.mean().item(), self.train_step)
                        print(g, f'Avg u: {g.mean().item()}')

                    for i in range(self.args.col_batch_size):
                        state = [state_proc_item[i].data.cpu().numpy(),
                                 state_space[i].data.cpu().numpy(),
                                 state_pack[i].data.cpu().numpy()]
                        state_ = [state_proc_item_[i].data.cpu().numpy(),
                                  state_space_[i].data.cpu().numpy(),
                                  state_pack_[i].data.cpu().numpy()]
                        action = [idx_io[i].item(),
                                  idx_s[i].item()]

                        reward = rewards[i].detach().data.cpu().numpy()
                        done = torch.count_nonzero(state_proc_item_) == 0

                        self.dqn.store_transition(state, action, reward, state_, done)

                    if (int(0) <= self.dqn.memory_counter) and (self.dqn.memory_counter % 4 == 0):
                        loss = self.dqn.learn()
                        self.train_step += 1

                    state_proc_item = state_proc_item_
                    state_cand_item = state_cand_item_
                    state_pack_col = state_pack_col_
                    state_space = state_space_
                    state_pack = state_pack_
            self.dqn.save_model(ckpt_dir + '/Q_{}_{}_{}.pth'.format(args.bin_length, args.bin_width, args.item_num)
                                    + datetime.now().strftime("%Y%m%d_%H%M%S"))

    def evaluate(self):
        dataset_eva = ItemDataset(args, eva=True)
        dataloader_eva = DataLoader(dataset_eva,
                                         batch_size=1,
                                         shuffle=False,
                                         num_workers=0)
        U = []
        iterator = tqdm(dataloader_eva, unit='Batch')
        for i_batch, sample_batched in enumerate(iterator):
            iterator.set_description('episode %i' % (i_batch))
            item = sample_batched
            state_proc_item = item[:, 0: args.item_num - args.cand_item_num, :].to(self.device)
            if args.cand_item_num != 0:
                state_cand_item = item[:, -args.cand_item_num:, :].to(self.device)
            else:
                state_cand_item = torch.zeros([1, 1, 3]).to(self.device)

            max_H = torch.sum(torch.max(item, dim=2)[0], dim=1)
            bin = torch.tensor([self.args.bin_length, self.args.bin_width]).unsqueeze(0).repeat(1, 1)
            state_space = torch.cat((torch.zeros([1, 3]), bin, max_H.unsqueeze(1), bin, max_H.unsqueeze(1)), dim=1).unsqueeze(1)
            state_space = torch.cat((state_space, torch.zeros([1, self.args.max_deal_space_num - 1, 9])), dim=1).to(self.device)
            state_pack = torch.zeros([1, min(args.item_num, args.max_deal_pack_num), 9], device=self.device)
            state_pack_col = torch.zeros([self.args.col_batch_size, args.item_num, 9], device=self.device)

            '''
            state_proc_item: (l, w, h) for each item in processing set
            state_cand_item: (l, w, h) for each item in candidate set
            state_pack: (x, y, z, x', y', w', l', w', h') for each item in packed set
            state_pack_col: (x, y, z, x', y', w', l', w', h') for each item in full packed set
            state_space: (x, y, z, x', y', w', l', w', h') for each space
            '''
            for step in range(self.args.item_num):

                action_, action_size = self.dqn.choose_action(state_proc_item, state_space, state_pack, evaluate=True)
                id = (action_ / action_size).long()
                action = action_ % action_size

                state_packs = self.dqn.rotate_pack(state_pack)
                state_spaces = self.dqn.rotate_space(state_space)
                state_packs_col = self.dqn.rotate_pack(state_pack_col)

                state_pack = torch.gather(state_packs,
                                           0,
                                           (id % 4).view(1, -1, 1, 1).repeat(1, 1, state_packs.size(2), state_packs.size(3))).squeeze(0)
                state_space = torch.gather(state_spaces,
                                          0,
                                          (id % 4).view(1, -1, 1, 1).repeat(1, 1, state_spaces.size(2), state_spaces.size(3))).squeeze(0)
                state_pack_col = torch.gather(state_packs_col,
                                              0,
                                              (id % 4).view(1, -1, 1, 1).repeat(1, 1, state_packs_col.size(2),
                                                                                state_packs_col.size(3))).squeeze(0)

                state_proc_item_, state_space_, state_pack_, state_pack_col_ = Pack.update_state(
                    state_proc_item,
                    state_space,
                    state_pack,
                    state_pack_col,
                    action,
                )
                if args.cand_item_num != 0:
                    action_cand = self.dqn.chose_action_cand(state_proc_item_, state_pack_, state_space_, state_cand_item)
                else:
                    action_cand = None
                state_proc_item_, state_cand_item_ = Pack.update_state_cand(state_proc_item_, state_space, state_cand_item, action, action_cand)

                if torch.count_nonzero(state_proc_item_) == 0:
                    V = torch.sum(torch.prod(state_pack_col_[:, :, 6:9], 2), dim=1)
                    H = torch.max(state_pack_col_[:, :, 5], dim=1)[0]
                    L, W = args.bin_length, args.bin_width
                    g = V / (L * W * H)
                    U.append(g.item())
                    print(f'Eps UR: {g.item()}, Avg UR: {sum(U)/len(U)}, Var: {np.var(U)}')

                state_proc_item = state_proc_item_
                state_cand_item = state_cand_item_
                state_space = state_space_
                state_pack = state_pack_
                state_pack_col = state_pack_col_

class QNetwork(nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, args):
        super(QNetwork, self).__init__()
        self.args = args
        self.device = torch.device('cuda:' + self.args.gpu_ids) if self.args.gpu_ids != '-1' else torch.device('cpu')
        self.item_encoder = ItemEncoder(hidden_size=args.hidden_size,
                                        inner_hidden_size=args.inner_hidden_size,
                                        attn_head_num=args.attn_head_num,
                                        attn_layer_num=args.attn_layer_num)
        self.pack_encoder = PackEncoder(hidden_size=args.hidden_size,
                                        inner_hidden_size=args.inner_hidden_size,
                                        attn_head_num=args.attn_head_num,
                                        attn_layer_num=args.attn_layer_num)
        self.space_decoder = SpaceDecoder(hidden_size=args.hidden_size,
                                          inner_hidden_size=args.inner_hidden_size,
                                          attn_head_num=args.attn_head_num,
                                          attn_layer_num=args.attn_layer_num)

    def forward(self, state_proc_item, state_space, state_pack):
        key_padding_mask_space = torch.all(state_space == 0, dim=2)
        key_padding_mask_pack = torch.all(state_pack == 0, dim=2)
        idx = torch.all(key_padding_mask_pack, dim=1)
        key_padding_mask_pack[:, 0] = torch.where(idx, torch.zeros_like(idx), key_padding_mask_pack[:, 0])

        h_i, state_item_allr = self.item_encoder.forward(state_proc_item)
        h_p = self.pack_encoder(state_pack, key_padding_mask_pack)
        h_s = self.space_decoder.forward(state_space, h_p, key_padding_mask_space, key_padding_mask_pack)
        n_i, n_s = h_i.size(1), h_s.size(1)
        score = torch.bmm(h_i, h_s.permute(0, 2, 1))

        if state_proc_item.size(1) > 0:
            less_than_mask = state_space[:, :, 6:9].unsqueeze(1).repeat(1, n_i, 1, 1) >= state_item_allr.unsqueeze(2).repeat(1, 1, n_s, 1)
            mask1 = less_than_mask.all(dim=-1)  # batch, n_i, n_s
            mask2 = torch.any(state_item_allr > 0, dim=2).unsqueeze(2).repeat(1, 1, n_s)
            mask3 = torch.any(state_space > 0, dim=2).unsqueeze(1).repeat(1, n_i, 1)
            score[~(mask1 * mask2 * mask3)] = torch.tensor(float('-inf')).to(state_proc_item.device)

        q = score.reshape(-1, n_i * n_s)
        idx = torch.all(torch.isinf(q), dim=1).unsqueeze(1).repeat(1, n_i * n_s)
        q = torch.where(idx, torch.zeros_like(q), q)

        return q

    def forward_cand(self, state_item, state_cand_item, state_space, h_s):
        h_cand, state_item_cand_allr = self.item_encoder.forward_cand(state_item, state_cand_item)
        n_cand, n_s = h_cand.size(1), h_s.size(1)
        score = torch.bmm(h_cand, h_s.permute(0, 2, 1))
        if torch.count_nonzero(state_cand_item) > 0:
            less_than_mask = state_space[:, :, 6:9].unsqueeze(1).repeat(1, n_cand, 1, 1) >= state_item_cand_allr.unsqueeze(2).repeat(1, 1, n_s, 1)
            mask1 = less_than_mask.all(dim=-1)  # batch, n_i, n_s
            mask2 = torch.any(state_item_cand_allr > 0, dim=2).unsqueeze(2).repeat(1, 1, n_s)
            mask3 = torch.any(state_space > 0, dim=2).unsqueeze(1).repeat(1, n_cand, 1)
            score[~(mask1 * mask2 * mask3)] = torch.tensor(float('-inf')).to(state_item.device)
        q_cand = torch.max(score, dim=2)[0]
        return q_cand


class ItemEncoder(nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, hidden_size=128, inner_hidden_size=512, attn_head_num=8, attn_layer_num=2):
        super(ItemEncoder, self).__init__()
        self.attn_layer_num = attn_layer_num
        self.attn_head_num = attn_head_num
        self.hidden_size = hidden_size
        self.embedding = nn.Sequential(
            nn.Linear(3, hidden_size),
            nn.ReLU(),
            nn.Linear(hidden_size, hidden_size)
        )
        self.MHAs = nn.ModuleList(
            [nn.MultiheadAttention(hidden_size, attn_head_num, batch_first=True) for _ in range(attn_layer_num)])
        self.FFs = nn.ModuleList([nn.Sequential(
            nn.Linear(hidden_size, inner_hidden_size),
            nn.ReLU(),
            nn.Linear(inner_hidden_size, hidden_size)
        ) for _ in range(attn_layer_num)])
        self.norm1s = nn.ModuleList([nn.LayerNorm(hidden_size) for _ in range(attn_layer_num)])
        self.norm2s = nn.ModuleList([nn.LayerNorm(hidden_size) for _ in range(attn_layer_num)])

    def forward(self, state_item):
        l = state_item.size(1)
        batch_size = state_item.size(0)
        if torch.count_nonzero(state_item) == 0:
            return torch.zeros([batch_size, 1, self.hidden_size]).to(state_item.device), None

        sequence = [0, 1, 2, 0, 2, 1, 1, 0, 2, 1, 2, 0, 2, 0, 1, 2, 1, 0]
        inx_allr = torch.tensor(sequence, dtype=torch.long).reshape(6, 3).cuda()
        inx_allr = inx_allr.repeat(l, 1).unsqueeze(0).repeat(batch_size, 1, 1)
        state_item_allr = state_item.repeat(1, 1, 6).reshape(batch_size, 6 * l, 3)
        state_item_allr = torch.gather(state_item_allr, 2, inx_allr)
        key_padding_mask = torch.all(state_item_allr == 0, dim=2)

        attn_mask = torch.eye(l, device='cuda:0').repeat(1, 6).reshape(6 * l, l)
        attn_mask = attn_mask.transpose(0, 1).repeat(1, 6).reshape(6 * l, 6 * l) - torch.eye(6 * l, device=state_item.device)
        attn_mask = attn_mask * (-1e8)
        attn_mask = attn_mask.unsqueeze(0).repeat(batch_size * self.attn_head_num, 1, 1)
        h = self.embedding(state_item_allr)
        for i in range(self.attn_layer_num):
            h = self.norm1s[i](h + self.MHAs[i](h, h, h, attn_mask=attn_mask, key_padding_mask=key_padding_mask)[0])
            h = self.norm2s[i](h + self.FFs[i](h))
        h_i = h
        return h_i, state_item_allr

    def forward_cand(self, state_proc_item, state_item_cand):
        l = state_proc_item.size(1)
        l_cand = state_item_cand.size(1)
        batch_size = state_proc_item.size(0)
        if torch.count_nonzero(state_proc_item) == 0 or torch.count_nonzero(state_item_cand) == 0:
            return torch.zeros([batch_size, 1, self.hidden_size]).to(state_proc_item.device), None

        sequence = [0, 1, 2, 0, 2, 1, 1, 0, 2, 1, 2, 0, 2, 0, 1, 2, 1, 0]
        inx_allr = torch.tensor(sequence, dtype=torch.long).reshape(6, 3).cuda()
        inx_allr = inx_allr.repeat(l, 1).unsqueeze(0).repeat(batch_size, 1, 1)
        state_item_allr = state_proc_item.repeat(1, 1, 6).reshape(batch_size, 6 * l, 3)
        state_item_allr = torch.gather(state_item_allr, 2, inx_allr)

        # cand_zero = torch.all(state_item_cand == 0, dim=2)
        # cand_zero_idx = torch.where(~cand_zero.squeeze())[0]  # 取反并获取索引
        # state_item_cand_nonzero = state_item_cand[:, ~cand_zero_idx.squeeze(), :]
        inx_allr = torch.tensor(sequence, dtype=torch.long).reshape(6, 3).cuda()
        inx_allr = inx_allr.repeat(l_cand, 1).unsqueeze(0).repeat(batch_size, 1, 1)
        state_item_cand_allr = state_item_cand.repeat(1, 1, 6).reshape(batch_size, 6 * l_cand, 3)
        state_item_cand_allr = torch.gather(state_item_cand_allr, 2, inx_allr)
        key_padding_mask = torch.all(state_item_allr == 0, dim=2)

        attn_mask = torch.eye(l, device='cuda:0').repeat(1, 6).reshape(6 * l, l)
        attn_mask = attn_mask.transpose(0, 1).repeat(1, 6).reshape(6 * l, 6 * l) - torch.eye(6 * l,
                                                                                             device=state_proc_item.device)
        attn_mask = attn_mask * (-1e8)
        attn_mask = attn_mask.unsqueeze(0).repeat(batch_size * self.attn_head_num, 1, 1)

        h = self.embedding(state_item_allr)
        h_cand = self.embedding(state_item_cand_allr)
        for i in range(self.attn_layer_num):
            h_cand = self.norm1s[i](h_cand + self.MHAs[i](h_cand, h, h, key_padding_mask=key_padding_mask)[0])
            h_cand = self.norm2s[i](h_cand + self.FFs[i](h_cand))

            h = self.norm1s[i](h + self.MHAs[i](h, h, h, attn_mask=attn_mask, key_padding_mask=key_padding_mask)[0])
            h = self.norm2s[i](h + self.FFs[i](h))
        return h_cand, state_item_cand_allr


class PackEncoder(nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, hidden_size=128, inner_hidden_size=512, attn_head_num=8, attn_layer_num=2):
        super(PackEncoder, self).__init__()
        self.attn_layer_num = attn_layer_num
        self.hidden_size = hidden_size
        self.embedding = nn.Sequential(
            nn.Linear(9, hidden_size),
            nn.ReLU(),
            nn.Linear(hidden_size, hidden_size)
        )
        self.MHAs = nn.ModuleList(
            [nn.MultiheadAttention(hidden_size, attn_head_num, batch_first=True) for _ in range(attn_layer_num)])
        self.FFs = nn.ModuleList([nn.Sequential(
            nn.Linear(hidden_size, inner_hidden_size),
            nn.ReLU(),
            nn.Linear(inner_hidden_size, hidden_size)
        ) for _ in range(attn_layer_num)])
        self.norm1s = nn.ModuleList([nn.LayerNorm(hidden_size) for _ in range(attn_layer_num)])
        self.norm2s = nn.ModuleList([nn.LayerNorm(hidden_size) for _ in range(attn_layer_num)])

    def forward(self, state_pack, key_padding_mask_pack):
        if torch.count_nonzero(state_pack) == 0:
            return torch.zeros([state_pack.size(0), state_pack.size(1), self.hidden_size]).cuda()
        h = self.embedding(state_pack)
        for i in range(self.attn_layer_num):
            h = self.norm1s[i](h + self.MHAs[i](h, h, h, key_padding_mask=key_padding_mask_pack)[0])
            h = self.norm2s[i](h + self.FFs[i](h))
        h_p = h
        return h_p


class SpaceDecoder(nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, hidden_size=128, inner_hidden_size=512, attn_head_num=8, attn_layer_num=2):
        super(SpaceDecoder, self).__init__()
        self.attn_layer_num = attn_layer_num
        self.embedding = nn.Sequential(
            nn.Linear(9, hidden_size),
            nn.ReLU(),
            nn.Linear(hidden_size, hidden_size)
        )
        self.MHA1s = nn.ModuleList(
            [nn.MultiheadAttention(hidden_size, attn_head_num, batch_first=True) for _ in range(attn_layer_num)])
        self.MHA2s = nn.ModuleList(
            [nn.MultiheadAttention(hidden_size, attn_head_num, batch_first=True) for _ in range(attn_layer_num)])
        self.FFs = nn.ModuleList([nn.Sequential(
            nn.Linear(hidden_size, inner_hidden_size),
            nn.ReLU(),
            nn.Linear(inner_hidden_size, hidden_size)
        ) for _ in range(attn_layer_num)])
        self.linears = nn.Sequential(
            nn.Linear(hidden_size, hidden_size),
            nn.LayerNorm(hidden_size),
            nn.ReLU(),
            nn.Linear(hidden_size, hidden_size)
        )
        self.norm1s = nn.ModuleList([nn.LayerNorm(hidden_size) for _ in range(attn_layer_num)])
        self.norm2s = nn.ModuleList([nn.LayerNorm(hidden_size) for _ in range(attn_layer_num)])
        self.norm3s = nn.ModuleList([nn.LayerNorm(hidden_size) for _ in range(attn_layer_num)])

    def forward(self, state_space, h_p, key_padding_mask_space, key_padding_mask_pack):
        h = self.embedding(state_space)
        for i in range(self.attn_layer_num):
            h = self.norm1s[i](h + self.MHA1s[i](h, h, h, key_padding_mask=key_padding_mask_space)[0])
            h = self.norm2s[i](h + self.MHA2s[i](h, h_p, h_p, key_padding_mask=key_padding_mask_pack)[0])
            h = self.norm3s[i](h + self.FFs[i](h))
        out = self.linears(h)
        return out


import random
class ReplayMemory:
    def __init__(self, memory_size=100000, per=False):
        self.states = {'proc_items': [None for _ in range(memory_size)],
                       'spaces': [None for _ in range(memory_size)],
                       'packs': [None for _ in range(memory_size)]}
        self.next_states = {'proc_items': [None for _ in range(memory_size)],
                       'spaces': [None for _ in range(memory_size)],
                       'packs': [None for _ in range(memory_size)]}
        self.actions = {
            'idx_io': np.zeros(memory_size),
            'idx_s': np.zeros(memory_size),
            'idx_cand': np.zeros(memory_size)
        }
        self.rewards = np.zeros(memory_size)
        self.terminals = np.zeros(memory_size)
        self.count = 0
        self.current = 0
        self.memory_size = memory_size
    def add(self, state, action, reward, next_state, terminal):
        self.rewards[self.current] = reward
        self.terminals[self.current] = terminal
        self.actions['idx_io'][self.current] = action[0]
        self.actions['idx_s'][self.current] = action[1]
        self.states['proc_items'][self.current] = state[0]
        self.states['spaces'][self.current] = state[1]
        self.states['packs'][self.current] = state[2]
        self.next_states['proc_items'][self.current] = next_state[0]
        self.next_states['spaces'][self.current] = next_state[1]
        self.next_states['packs'][self.current] = next_state[2]
        self.current = (self.current + 1) % self.memory_size
        self.count += 1
    def sample(self, batch_size):
        state_batch = {'proc_items': [None for _ in range(batch_size)],
                       'spaces': [None for _ in range(batch_size)],
                       'packs': [None for _ in range(batch_size)]}
        reward_batch = []
        action_batch = {
            'idx_io': np.zeros(batch_size),
            'idx_s': np.zeros(batch_size),
        }
        terminal_batch = []
        next_state_batch = {'proc_items': [None for _ in range(batch_size)],
                       'spaces': [None for _ in range(batch_size)],
                       'packs': [None for _ in range(batch_size)]}
        for i in range(batch_size):
            data_index = random.randint(0, self.current-1 if self.count < self.memory_size else self.memory_size-1)
            reward_batch.append(self.rewards[data_index])
            action_batch['idx_io'][i] = self.actions['idx_io'][data_index]
            action_batch['idx_s'][i] = self.actions['idx_s'][data_index]
            terminal_batch.append(self.terminals[data_index])
            state_batch['proc_items'][i] = self.states['proc_items'][data_index]
            state_batch['spaces'][i] = self.states['spaces'][data_index]
            state_batch['packs'][i] = self.states['packs'][data_index]
            next_state_batch['proc_items'][i] = self.next_states['proc_items'][data_index]
            next_state_batch['spaces'][i] = self.next_states['spaces'][data_index]
            next_state_batch['packs'][i] = self.next_states['packs'][data_index]
        return state_batch, reward_batch, action_batch, next_state_batch, terminal_batch
